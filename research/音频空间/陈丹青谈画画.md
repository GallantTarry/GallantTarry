# 陈丹青谈画画

<tukuai-player url="media/videos/陈丹青谈画画.mp4" title="陈丹青谈画画"></tukuai-player>


这段短片截取自陈丹青在纽约大都会艺术博物馆前的深夜直播对谈。面对年轻人关于“要不要画画”、“怎么看画”以及“艺术与装逼”的提问，他用一贯辛辣、真实又直击本质的言语，道出了艺术最原始的本能与热爱。



**英文翻译 (English Transcript):**


"Those are questions only asked by people without talent, right? If you love it, you just paint, right?

**Don't listen to an old man or some painter telling you what you should or shouldn't do. It's pure instinct—when you're hungry, you have to eat; when you love painting, you just paint. Don't ask anyone, just go paint.** It seems you don't really love painting that much. **If you truly love painting, holy shit, nothing can hold you back.** If you genuinely adore this, you'd be painting all year round.

**When you look at a painting, you'd better be just like a dog—have no knowledge, have no brain.** But then again, dogs don't look at paintings; only humans do.

*(Q: How do you view the relationship between 'posing / pretentiousness' and art?)*  
'Posing' (Zhuangbi) is just a modern buzzword, basically meaning pretending to look very refined and elegant, right? **Well, art is posing! Every era and every country invents new words to describe art. I actually love this word. I've been posing ever since I was a kid, and I posed my way to who I am today, hahaha."**



**中文原话：**


“都是没有才能的人问的话，对吧。你喜欢你画就是了嘛，对吧。

**不要听一个老头子、一个画家说要怎么样怎么样。这个本能呀，你肚子饿了你就得吃饭，你喜欢你就要画画。不要问任何人，就画就是了。** 看样子你不是很有喜欢画画。**喜欢画画，我操，这拦不住的。** 就是好这个，就一年到头在那画画。

**你看画的时候最好跟条狗一样，就不要有知识，不要有脑子。** 但是狗又不会看画，只有人会看画。

（问：怎么看待装逼和艺术的关系？）  
‘装逼’是现在人想出来的一个词啦，就是好像装得很雅的样子，大概是这个意思吧。**那艺术就是装逼啊！每个时代、每个国家会有新的词来形容艺术。我喜欢‘装逼’这个词，我从小装逼，装成了现在，哈哈哈。**”
