《Blowin' in the Wind》是鲍勃·迪伦（Bob Dylan）于1962年创作的传世经典，被誉为20世纪最伟大的民谣抗议歌曲之一。歌词通过一系列充满诗意而深刻的发问，探讨了和平、自由、战争与人性的永恒命题。



**英文歌词 (English Lyrics):**


"**How many roads must a man walk down, before you call him a man?**
How many seas must a white dove sail, before she sleeps in the sand?
Yes, and how many times must the cannonballs fly, before they're forever banned?
**The answer, my friend, is blowin' in the wind, the answer is blowin' in the wind.**

Yes, and how many years can a mountain exist, before it is washed to the sea?
**Yes, and how many years can some people exist, before they're allowed to be free?**
**Yes, and how many times can a man turn his head, and pretend that he just doesn't see?**
**The answer, my friend, is blowin' in the wind, the answer is blowin' in the wind.**

Yes, and how many times must a man look up, before he can see the sky?
Yes, and how many ears must one man have, before he can hear people cry?
**Yes, and how many deaths will it take 'til he knows, that too many people have died?**
**The answer, my friend, is blowin' in the wind, the answer is blowin' in the wind.**"

炮弹究竟还要呼啸飞过多少次，才会被永远地禁止？
**朋友，答案就在风中飘荡，答案就在风中飘荡。**

一座高山要屹立多少年，才会被冲刷消融汇入大海？
**一些人究竟要苦熬多少年，才能获得本该属于他们的自由？**
**一个人究竟还要多少次转过头去，假装自己什么都没有看见？**
**朋友，答案就在风中飘荡，答案就在风中飘荡。**

一个人要仰望多少次，才能看清这片广袤的天空？

**到底还要牺牲多少条鲜活的生命，人们才会幡然醒悟：逝去的人实在太多了？**
**朋友，答案就在风中飘荡，答案就在风中飘荡。**”
