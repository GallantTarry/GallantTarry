# 罗素给世界的留言

**提问者 (Interviewer):**
Lord Russell, suppose this film were to be looked at by our descendants, like a Dead Sea scroll in a thousand years' time. What would you think it's worth telling that generation about the life you've lived and the lessons you've learned from it?
罗素勋爵，假定这段录像将被我们的后人看到，就像一千年后的人们看死海古卷那样。关于您的一生以及您从中得到的教诲，您觉得有什么是值得对那一代人说的？

**伯特兰·罗素 (Bertrand Russell):**
I should like to say two things, one intellectual and one moral.
我想说两点，一点关乎智慧，一点关乎道德。

The intellectual thing I should want to say is this:
我想说的关乎智慧的一点是：

When you are studying any matter, or considering any philosophy, ask yourself only what are the facts and what is the truth that the facts bear out.
无论你是在研究任何事物，还是在思考任何哲学理论，只问你自己：事实是什么，以及这些事实所证实的真理是什么。

Never let yourself be diverted either by what you wish to believe, or by what you think would have beneficent social effects if it were believed.
永远不要被自己所更愿意相信的，或者认为人们相信了之后会对社会有益的东西所误导。

But look only, and solely, at what are the facts. That is the intellectual thing that I should wish to say.
只是单单去审视，什么才是事实。这是我想说的关乎智慧的一点。

The moral thing I should wish to say to them is very simple.
我想对他们说的关乎道德的一点十分简单。

I should say: Love is wise, hatred is foolish.
我会说：爱是明智的，恨是愚蠢的。

In this world, which is getting more and more closely interconnected, we have to learn to tolerate each other, we have to learn to put up with the fact that some people say things that we don't like.
在这个日益紧密相连的世界里，我们必须学会容忍彼此，我们必须学会接受这样一个事实：总会有人说出我们不爱听的话。

We can only live together in that way.
我们只能以这种方式共存。

But if we are to live together, and not die together, we must learn a kind of charity and a kind of tolerance, which is absolutely vital to the continuation of human life on this planet.
如果我们想要共存，而非共亡，我们就必须学会一种宽容与忍让，因为它们对于人类在这个星球上的存续是至关重要的。
