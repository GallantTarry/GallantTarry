# 罗素给世界的留言

<tukuai-player url="media/videos/罗素给世界的留言.mp4" title="罗素给世界的留言"></tukuai-player>


这段著名的访谈出自1959年英国BBC对87岁高龄的哲学家、数学家伯特兰·罗素（Bertrand Russell）的电视专访。当被问及若这段影像在千年后被后世发现、最想给未来人类留下什么告诫时，他给出了关于“智慧”与“道德”的两段真知灼见。



**英文原话 (English Transcript):**


"Lord Russell, suppose this film were to be looked at by our descendants, like a Dead Sea scroll in a thousand years' time. What would you think it's worth telling that generation about the life you've lived and the lessons you've learned from it?

I should like to say two things, one intellectual and one moral.

The intellectual thing I should want to say is this: **When you are studying any matter, or considering any philosophy, ask yourself only what are the facts and what is the truth that the facts bear out.** Never let yourself be diverted either by what you wish to believe, or by what you think would have beneficent social effects if it were believed. **But look only, and solely, at what are the facts.** That is the intellectual thing that I should wish to say.

The moral thing I should wish to say to them is very simple. I should say: **Love is wise, hatred is foolish.** In this world, which is getting more and more closely interconnected, we have to learn to tolerate each other, we have to learn to put up with the fact that some people say things that we don't like. We can only live together in that way. **But if we are to live together, and not die together, we must learn a kind of charity and a kind of tolerance, which is absolutely vital to the continuation of human life on this planet.**"



**中文翻译：**


“罗素勋爵，假定这段录像将被我们的后人看到，就像一千年后的人们看死海古卷那样。关于您的一生以及您从中得到的教诲，您觉得有什么是值得对那一代人说的？

我想说两点，一点关乎智慧，一点关乎道德。

我想说的关乎智慧的一点是：**无论你是在研究任何事物，还是在思考任何哲学理论，只问你自己：事实是什么，以及这些事实所证实的真理是什么。** 永远不要被自己所更愿意相信的，或者认为人们相信了之后会对社会有益的东西所误导。**只是单单去审视，什么才是事实。** 这是我想说的关乎智慧的一点。

我想对他们说的关乎道德的一点十分简单。我会说：**爱是明智的，恨是愚蠢的。** 在这个日益紧密相连的世界里，我们必须学会容忍彼此，我们必须学会接受这样一个事实：总会有人说出我们不爱听的话。我们只能以这种方式共存。**如果我们想要共存，而非共亡，我们就必须学会一种宽容与忍让，因为它们对于人类在这个星球上的存续是至关重要的。**”
