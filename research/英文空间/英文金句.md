# 英文金句


> **The memories I value most, I don't ever see them fading.**
> 
> 那些我最珍视的记忆从未褪色。
> 
> 

> **Weak people revenge. Strong people forgive. Intelligent people ignore.**
> 
> 弱者报复，强者原谅，智者无视。
> 
>   

> **A smile cures the wounding of a frown.**
> 
> 一个微笑能治愈皱眉带来的伤害。
> 
>   

> **The truest wisdom is a resolute determination.**
> 
> 真正的才智是刚毅的志向。
> 
>   

> **You can act like a man.**
> 
> 你可以像一个男人一样。
> 
>   

> **Information is everything.**
> 
> 信息就是一切。
> 
>   


> **Hope is a good thing, maybe the best of things, and no good thing ever dies.**
> 
> 希望是美好的，也许是人间至善，而美好的事物永不消逝。
> 
>   

> **To strive, to seek, to find, and not to yield.**
> 
> 去奋斗，去追求，去发现，永不屈服。
> 
>   

> **The question isn't who is going to let me; it's who is going to stop me.**
> 
> 问题不在于谁会允许我，而在于谁能阻止我。
> 
>   

> **We are not rich by what we possess but by what we can do without.**
> 
> 我们之富有，不在于拥有多少，而在于能放下多少。（或：衡量富有的标准不是我们拥有多少，而是我们能舍弃什么。）
> 
>   

> **In this one moment, you know that you're not a sad story. You are alive.**
> 
> 就在这一刻你明白了，你不是一个悲惨的故事，你正充满活力地活着。
> 
>   

> **If you were waiting for the opportune moment, that was it.**
> 
> 如果你一直在等待绝佳的时机，刚才那就是了。
> 
>   

> **It's a place where the limits of reality are your own imagination.**
> 
> 在这里，现实无界，想象无边。
> 
> 


> **Patience. The windmill never strays in search of the wind.**
> 
> 耐心等待。风车从不跑去找风。
> 
> 

> **Grief sharpens the mind.**
> 
> 悲伤使人格外敏锐。
> 
> 

> **The memories I value most, I don't ever see them fading.**
> 
> 那些我最珍视的记忆从未褪色。
>
