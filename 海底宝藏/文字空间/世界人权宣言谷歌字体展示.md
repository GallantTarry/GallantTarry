
# 世界人权宣言谷歌字体展示

## **Whereas recognition of the inherent dignity**  


**No one shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest, detention or exile.**  
任何人不得遭受任意逮捕、拘禁或放逐。

**Everyone is entitled in full equality to a fair and public hearing by an independent and impartial tribunal, in the determination of his rights and obligations and of any criminal charge against him.**  
人人完全平等地有权由一个独立而公正的法庭进行公正和公开的审理，以确定其权利和义务，并裁定对其提出的任何刑事指控。

**No one shall be subjected to arbitrary interference with his privacy, family, home or correspondence, nor to attacks upon his honour and reputation. Everyone has the right to the protection of the law against such interference or attacks.**  
任何人的隐私、家庭、住宅或通信不得受到任意干涉，其荣誉和名誉也不得受到攻击。人人有权获得法律保护，以免遭受此类干涉或攻击。

**Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion; this right includes freedom to change his religion or belief, and freedom, either alone or in community with others and in public or private, to manifest his religion or belief in teaching, practice, worship and observance.**  
人人有思想、良心和宗教自由的权利；这一权利包括改变宗教或信仰的自由，以及单独或与他人共同、公开或私下通过教导、实践、礼拜和遵守宗教仪式来表达其宗教或信仰的自由。

**Everyone has the right to freedom of opinion and expression; this right includes freedom to hold opinions without interference and to seek, receive and impart information and ideas through any media and regardless of frontiers.**  
人人有发表意见和表达的自由；这一权利包括持有意见而不受干涉的自由，以及通过任何媒介、不受国界限制地寻求、接收和传播信息与思想的自由。

**Everyone has the right to rest and leisure, including reasonable limitation of working hours and periodic holidays with pay.**  
人人有休息和闲暇的权利，包括合理限制工作时间和定期享有带薪休假。
